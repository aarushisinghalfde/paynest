id: KB-0220
title: Full and final settlement process
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Employees > select the employee > Exit > Start full and final.
2. Enter the last working day; Payce adds pending salary, leave encashment and deductions.
3. Review each line and add any one-time items.
4. Click Submit for approval; after approval the settlement is paid in the selected payroll run.
5. Download the settlement letter from Exit > Documents.
