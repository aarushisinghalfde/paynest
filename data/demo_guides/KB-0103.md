---
id: KB-0103
title: Resetting passwords and SSO sign-in problems
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. On the sign-in page click Forgot password and enter your work email.
2. Open the reset link within 30 minutes and set a new password.
3. An admin can reset it for a user under Settings > Users > select the user > Reset password.
4. If your company uses single sign-on, sign in with the SSO button; your IT team manages that password.
5. Locked after 5 attempts? Wait 15 minutes or ask an admin to unlock the user under Settings > Users.
