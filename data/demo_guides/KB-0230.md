---
id: KB-0230
title: Arrears and retro-pay adjustments
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Update the employee's pay with the effective date in the past under Employees > Pay details > Revise.
2. Payce calculates the difference for each past month automatically.
3. Review it under Payroll > Run payroll > Arrears before submitting the run.
4. The arrears show as a separate line on the payslip.
