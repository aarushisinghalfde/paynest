id: KB-0203
title: WPS: bank routing code and employee card setup
region: UAE
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Settings > WPS > Agent and enter your WPS agent (bank or exchange house).
2. For each employee open Employees > select the employee > Pay details.
3. Enter the employee's bank routing code and card or account number.
4. Click Save; the employee is included in the next SIF file.
