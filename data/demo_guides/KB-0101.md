---
id: KB-0101
title: How to run monthly payroll
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Payroll > Run payroll and pick the pay month.
2. Check the Inputs tab: new joiners, leavers, attendance and one-time payments must show "Complete".
3. Click Preview to see the payroll register; fix any row marked in red.
4. Click Submit for approval. The payroll approver gets an email and approves it under Payroll > Approvals.
5. After approval, click Lock payroll. Payslips and the bank file are generated.
6. Download the bank file from Payroll > Bank files and upload it to your bank portal.
