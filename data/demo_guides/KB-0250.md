region: ALL

1. Update the Payce app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Sign out and sign in again with your work email.
3. Payslips not showing? They appear only after payroll for that month is locked.
4. Still stuck: clear the app's storage (Android) or reinstall it (iPhone), then sign in again.
