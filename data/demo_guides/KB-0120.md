title: Invoices, GST and billing cycles
region: IN
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices to see and download every invoice.
2. To add or change your GSTIN, go to Settings > Billing > Company details and click Edit.
3. Invoices are raised on the 1st of each month for the previous month's active employees.
4. Change the billing email under Settings > Billing > Contacts.
