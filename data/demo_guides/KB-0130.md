title: Custom report builder
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Reports > Report builder > New report.
2. Pick a data source (Payroll, Employees, Attendance or Leave).
3. Drag the columns you need, then add filters (pay month, department, location).
4. Click Run to preview, then Save to reuse it or Export to Excel or CSV.
5. To share it, open the saved report and click Share with roles.
