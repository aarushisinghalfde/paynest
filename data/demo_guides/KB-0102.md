id: KB-0102
title: Adding a new employee (single and bulk upload)
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
Single employee:
1. Go to Employees > Add employee.
2. Fill in Personal, Job and Pay details; fields marked * are required.
3. Click Save. The employee appears in the next payroll run.
Bulk upload:
1. Go to Employees > Import > Download template.
2. Fill one row per employee and save the file as .xlsx.
3. Upload it under Employees > Import, review the preview and click Import.
