id: KB-0105
title: Downloading payslips in bulk
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Payroll > Payslips.
2. Pick the pay month.
3. Select all employees (tick the box at the top of the list) or filter by department.
4. Click Download and choose ZIP (one PDF per employee) or a single combined PDF.
5. Payslips are available only after the payroll for that month is locked.
