---
id: KB-0110
title: Attendance integration setup (biometric and HRIS connectors)
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Settings > Integrations > Attendance.
2. Choose your device or HRIS from the list and click Connect.
3. Enter the connection details your vendor gave you and click Test connection.
4. Map device user ids to employee codes (Upload mapping or match by employee code).
5. Set the sync time; attendance appears under Attendance > Daily view after the first sync.
