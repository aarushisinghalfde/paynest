---
id: KB-0135
title: Bank file formats: NEFT, RTGS and bulk transfer
region: IN
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Settings > Payroll > Bank file and choose your bank and format (NEFT, RTGS or bulk transfer).
2. Enter your company debit account under Settings > Payroll > Company bank.
3. After locking payroll, download the file from Payroll > Bank files.
4. Upload the file to your bank's corporate portal; do not edit it in Excel, which can change the number formats.
