id: KB-0144
title: PF: generating and uploading the ECR file
region: IN
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Lock the month's payroll first.
2. Go to Statutory > PF > ECR and pick the month.
3. Click Generate ECR and review the summary.
4. Download the .txt file and upload it on the EPFO employer portal under Payment > ECR Upload.
