---
id: KB-0104
title: Configuring leave policies and carry-forward
region: ALL
language: en
source: demo
---
1. Go to Leave > Policies > New policy.
2. Name the leave type, set the yearly entitlement and the accrual (monthly or yearly).
3. Under Carry-forward, set the maximum days carried into the new year and the expiry date.
4. Assign the policy to employees under Leave > Policies > Assign (by department, location or grade).
5. Click Save. Balances update at the next accrual.
